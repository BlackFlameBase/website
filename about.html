01 · ORIGIN

The Origin

Black Flame developed from the attempt to name and organize recurring realities of creative life: creative force, formation, direction, expression, expenditure, recovery, return, meaningful work, and the long responsibility of maintaining a relationship with creation.

The institution is intended to remain accountable to practice. Its ideas are carried through study, refinement, testing, creative work, and continued development rather than existing only as abstract propositions.

03 · EMBODIMENT

Proof of Embodiment

Black Flame's legitimacy depends upon embodiment. The institution must demonstrate its principle through its creators, practices, works, standards, continuity, and capacity to carry creative fire outward.

Founder links therefore function as evidence: visitors can move from institutional claims into the actual work and continuing practices of the people responsible for the system.
